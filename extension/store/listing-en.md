# Snapt: screenshots and image tools

**Short description** (from the manifest)

Screenshots of an area or a whole page with arrows, text and blur. Save images as PNG or JPG, Alt+click to zoom into pixels.

**Category**: Productivity (Chrome) / Photos, Music & Videos (Firefox)

**Description**

Snapt is a fast, open-source screenshot tool, now in your browser. No account, no cloud, no tracking: your screenshots never leave your computer.

TAKE A SCREENSHOT
• Select an area: drag over the page, or click a card, an image or a code block to take just that element (Alt+Shift+S)
• The visible part of the page (Alt+Shift+V)
• The whole page: Snapt scrolls it and glues it into one image, with sticky headers shown only once (Alt+Shift+P)
• A screen or another app's window, through the browser's sharing dialog

ANNOTATE
• Pen, line, arrow, rectangle, ellipse, marker, text and numbered steps
• Blur or pixelate passwords, emails and faces
• Ten colors or any color, filled shapes, adjustable sizes
• Move or delete any shape, undo and redo
• Shapes are drawn by the same code as the Snapt desktop app, so they look identical

SHARE
• Copy to the clipboard with Ctrl+C, or save as PNG or JPG

IMAGE TOOLS
• Right-click any image: "Save image as PNG" or "Save image as JPG". WebP, AVIF and SVG included; the conversion happens on your computer
• Alt+click any image to open it over the page: zoom with the wheel down to a pixel grid, drag to pan, rotate with R
